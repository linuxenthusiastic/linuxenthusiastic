Hi, I'm **Santiago** — a Software Engineer passionate about building secure, scalable, and high-performance systems.

I enjoy solving complex engineering challenges, designing robust architectures, and understanding how systems work under the hood. My interests span software engineering, cybersecurity, blockchain, and artificial intelligence, where I continuously explore cutting-edge technologies and best practices.

I'm especially passionate about **blockchain security**, **smart contract development**, decentralized systems, and the cryptographic foundations that make Web3 possible. I enjoy studying blockchain protocols, consensus mechanisms, EVM internals, Solana, smart contract auditing, and secure decentralized application (dApp) development.

Cybersecurity is another major focus of mine. I enjoy offensive security, threat modeling, secure software design, and understanding how attackers think in order to build resilient applications.

Recently, I've become deeply interested in **AI-assisted software engineering**. I enjoy optimizing development workflows using AI agents, experimenting with autonomous coding assistants, and integrating the latest AI technologies into real-world engineering processes to improve productivity and software quality.

Outside of coding, I'm a Linux enthusiast who enjoys working in terminal-first environments, automating workflows, and continuously improving my development setup.
